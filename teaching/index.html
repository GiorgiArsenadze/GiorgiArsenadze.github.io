New York University

General Physics II: GP2_PHYS-UA_12 — 2023,2024

Role: Clinical Assistant Professor

Description

This course is the second half of a two-part introduction to physics intended primarily for pre-professional students and for those majoring in a science other than physics. Topics include waves, electric fields, current, magnetic fields, images, and lenses. The course uses high school algebra, geometry, and trigonometry, plus some calculus. Calculus will be present in lecture, homework, and exams. Algebra, geometry, and trigonometry are essential.

New York University

General Physics I: GP1_PHYS-UA_11 — 2023

Role: Clinical Assistant Professor

Description

This course is first half of a two-part introduction to physics intended primarily for pre-professional students and for those majoring in a science other than physics. Top- ics include position, velocity, acceleration, force, Newton’s laws of motion, work, en- ergy, torque, gravitation, fluid, oscillation, and thermodynamics. The course uses high school algebra, geometry and trigonometry, vectors, and some calculus. Calculus will be present in lectures, homeworks and exams. The algebra, geometry, and trigonometry are absolutely essential.

New York University

Dynamics, General Physics I-II, Physics III, Cosmology, The Universe, Introductory Experimental Physics I, Intermediate Experimental Physics II, Observational astronomy — 2019-2025

Role: Teaching and Course Assistant

Description

Over the past six years, I have had the opportunity to teach a wide range of physics courses, spanning both theoretical and experimental areas. These include core subjects such as Dynamics, General Physics I and II, and Physics III, as well as more specialized courses like Cosmology and The Universe. I have also guided students in hands-on learning through Introductory Experimental Physics I, Intermediate Experimental Physics II, and Observational Astronomy. This experience has helped me grow as a lecturer and leader, improving my communication, organization, and mentoring skills. It has also given me valuable skills including problem-solving, teamwork, project management, and the ability to explain complex concepts clearly.
